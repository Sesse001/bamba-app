How It Works

1

Create Your Profile

Tell us your province, age group, and home language. This helps us give you the right translations.

2

Translate Daily

Receive phrases in English and translate them into your home language. Pick the context — casual, formal, or slang.

3

Review & Verify

Help verify other contributors' translations. Your reviews build trust in the platform.

4

See Your Impact

Track your contributions, see community activity across provinces, and watch the language library grow.

How We Build Trust

Low Confidence

One contributor has submitted this translation. More reviews needed.

Medium Confidence

Multiple contributors agree on similar translations.

High Confidence

Strong agreement across reviewers from different provinces.

Research Ecosystem

Bamba's mission is inspired by and aligned with South Africa's leading language research institutions.

📚

SADiLaR

South African Centre for Digital Language Resources

South Africa's national research infrastructure for digital language resources. Develops, preserves, and shares digital language technologies for all official languages.

Visit SADiLaR →
🚀

ESCALATOR

SADiLaR's Flagship Capacity Development Programme

Builds an inclusive community of practice in Digital Humanities. Provides training, mentorship, and digital skills development for researchers, students, and educators.

Visit ESCALATOR →

Bamba is an independent research and language technology initiative. Our work is aligned with the missions of SADiLaR and the ESCALATOR programme. Any formal partnerships will be announced if established.
